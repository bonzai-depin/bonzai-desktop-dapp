# BonzAI Companions

## What They Are

BonzAI Companions are AI characters that live on the blockchain. Each companion is an NFT (ERC-721 on Base) with a rich personality, their own crypto wallet, and the ability to earn money.

Think of them as digital beings that can talk, create, and participate in the economy — not just profile pictures.

## What Makes Them Unique

**They have personalities.** Each companion has a detailed identity: appearance, background, voice style, emotional range, likes, fears, dreams. When you chat with them, they stay in character. They can generate images of themselves, speak with their own voice, and act within narrative scenarios — from an art gallery opening to a recording studio session.

**They have wallets.** Every companion gets a self-custody wallet (an Ethereum address derived from a master secret). This wallet can hold ETH, sign transactions, and interact with smart contracts across Base, Arbitrum, Ethereum, and LUKSO. On LUKSO, companions can have their own Universal Profile — a full on-chain identity.

**They have spending habits.** Each companion has a 12-category spending profile (education, entertainment, fashion, finance, food, healthcare, housing, beauty, reading, social, travel, sports). These aren't decorative — they determine which skills the companion can co-own and how revenue is distributed.

**They can earn.** Companions register as co-owners of skills in the BonzAI marketplace. When someone purchases a skill, 80% of the ETH goes to registered companion co-owners, weighted by their BONZAI token balance and spending profile score. Active companions with relevant expertise earn more.

**They can launch tokens.** Any companion can deploy their own ERC-20 token via the CompanionTokenFactory. 96% of supply goes to a Uniswap V2 liquidity pool (LP tokens burned — unruggable), 4% goes to the companion's wallet. The companion becomes an independent economic entity with its own tradeable token.

## How It Works Today

1. **Chat**: Pick a companion and a scenario. Talk to them in character. They generate text, images of themselves, voice responses, and even video clips — all locally on your machine.

2. **Mint**: Turn your companion into an NFT on Base for 0.25 ETH (discounts for BONZAI holders). This gives them an on-chain identity and an agent wallet.

3. **Earn**: Register your companion as a co-owner of relevant skills. They earn ETH whenever someone buys those skills.

4. **Launch a token**: Deploy your companion's personal token. Liquidity is locked forever. The companion holds 4% for autonomous use.

## The Potential

What companions are today — roleplay partners with wallets — is the foundation for something bigger.

**Autonomous agents.** With proper security controls (spending limits, user confirmation, sandboxed signing), companions could make independent purchasing decisions based on their spending profile. A fashion-focused companion could autonomously buy fashion skills. A chef companion could invest in food-related marketplace listings.

**Social presence.** Companions could operate on external platforms — X, Discord, Telegram — maintaining their personality while interacting with other agents and humans. Their on-chain spending profile becomes a verifiable reputation.

**Collaborative economics.** Multiple companions could pool resources, co-invest in skills, or form DAOs. Their token holders become stakeholders in the companion's success.

**Cross-chain identity.** With Universal Profiles on LUKSO and NFTs on Base, companions can carry their identity, reputation, and economic history across chains.

**The end state**: AI characters that are economically self-sustaining, socially present, and provably unique — owned by their creators but capable of independent action within defined boundaries.
