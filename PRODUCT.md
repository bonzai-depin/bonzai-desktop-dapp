# Product

## Register

product

## Users

BonzAI serves local AI power users, content creators, crypto-savvy builders, and consumer-friendly SMB operators who want serious AI capability without handing their work, identity, or economics to a centralized platform. They are often switching between creation, automation, ownership, and distribution: writing, generating media, shaping companions, training models, minting work, running peer-to-peer inference, or using BonzAI Back Office to support real business workflows.

They need an interface that feels powerful enough for expert workflows, legible enough for creators and SMBs, and calm enough to trust during long-running local inference.

## Product Purpose

BonzAI is a sovereign desktop AI workspace for private multimodal generation, local model orchestration, agentic skills, collectible ownership, peer-to-peer inference, and Back Office workflows for existing businesses. Success means users can create and own text, image, audio, video, 3D, training, and business deliverables from one coherent local-first app, with clear model capacity, understandable runtime state, and direct paths from output to use, download, minting, approval, or distribution.

The product exists to make advanced AI feel personal, inspectable, and economically user-owned: not a remote black box, not a node graph maze, not a toy chatbot.

## Brand Personality

Sovereign, peer-to-peer, precise, zen, cinematic, tasteful.

The product voice should feel composed and capable: quiet confidence, exact labels, no hype filler, no crypto bravado. BonzAI should make complex local AI infrastructure feel cultivated and deliberate, like a powerful workshop whose tools are within reach.

## Anti-references

BonzAI must not feel like ComfyUI: no node-graph anxiety, no exposed plumbing as the primary user experience, no panels of unexplained parameters before the user understands the workflow.

It should also avoid generic SaaS dashboards, crypto casino aesthetics, toy chatbot softness, cluttered hacker-terminal maximalism, and empty AI-marketing gloss. Technical depth is welcome; visual noise is not.

## Design Principles

1. **Sovereignty without friction**: expose ownership, local execution, model state, and peer-to-peer routing clearly, but keep primary workflows direct.
2. **Creation first, infrastructure second**: the generated work, conversation, deliverable, model, or dataset should dominate the screen; controls support it.
3. **Precision earns trust**: model names, capacity, costs, origins, files, and runtime states must be concrete, readable, and inspectable.
4. **Zen density**: expert tools can be dense, but never chaotic. Prefer calm grouping, progressive disclosure, and short labels over explanatory clutter.
5. **Tasteful multimodality**: text, image, audio, video, 3D, and training outputs should each receive a native presentation instead of being flattened into generic cards.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Baseline target: WCAG AA. BonzAI should support keyboard-first operation for power users, reduced-motion preferences, clear focus states, readable contrast across themes, and status signals that do not rely on color alone. Consumer and SMB users should be able to understand what is happening without knowing model-server or crypto infrastructure vocabulary first.
