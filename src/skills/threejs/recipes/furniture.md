# Furniture Patterns (Table/Chair/Shelf/Chest)
- Table: BoxGeometry top + 4x CylinderGeometry legs at corners
- Chair: flat seat + tall thin back + 4 legs (back legs taller for support)
- Shelf/bookcase: 2x vertical sides + N horizontal shelves + BoxGeometry books with varied height/color
- Chest: BoxGeometry body + slightly wider lid, CylinderGeometry hinges, small lock detail
- Drawer: BoxGeometry body + front panel + SphereGeometry knob
- Materials: wood(color:0x8B5A2B, roughness:0.7), dark wood(0x3e2723), metal accents(metalness:0.9), fabric(roughness:1.0)
- Vary: rustic/elegant/modern, ornate carvings(ExtrudeGeometry), metal brackets, cushions, open/closed
